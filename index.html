Junhyung Kim

I'm an undergraduate student in Applied Artificial Intelligence at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU).

I am currently a research intern at the Visual Information Processing Lab at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Joonseok Lee, where I conduct research on video understanding and multimodal fusion.

Previously, I was a researcher at the Data & Language Intelligence Laboratory (DLI), advised by Prof. YunSeok Choi, and at the Digital Environment Human Behavior Lab (DEHUB), advised by Prof. Daeho Lee, at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU).

I am honored to have been selected as a Presidential Science Scholar, and I am always open to collaboration and research discussions.
